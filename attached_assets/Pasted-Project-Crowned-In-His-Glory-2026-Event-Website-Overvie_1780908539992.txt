Project: Crowned In His Glory 2026 Event Website

Overview

Build a premium event website for The Church of Pentecost – Post, Elizabeth District Youth Ministry for the event:

Crowned In His Glory 2026

The website must have:

* Modern luxury design
* Gold and black theme inspired by the uploaded ticket
* Online ticket purchasing
* Digital ticket generation
* QR code generation
* QR code scanning and validation system
* Admin dashboard
* Mobile responsive design
* Event analytics
* Attendee management

The website should feel like a premium awards ceremony, royal gala, and Christian excellence event.
